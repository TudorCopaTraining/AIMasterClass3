Module 3: AI Tools & Automation Platforms

Practical Task: The Vendor Evaluation & Scoring Matrix

Interactive Workshop Challenge

Applying the principle of "Start with the problem, not the technology," you must now evaluate three competing AI vendor solutions for a growing hospitality enterprise. Each vendor has different trade-offs across Cost, Features, Security, User Experience (UX), and Support.

Your Task: Review the vendor profiles below, assign importance weights (1 to 5) to each criterion based on business priorities, score each vendor (1 to 10), and justify your final recommendation. Once completed, you can check your evaluation or reveal the model solution!

Competing Vendor Profiles

Vendor A: Nexus GuestBot

Strengths: Outstanding UI/UX, lightning-fast setup, highly intuitive for front-desk staff.

Weaknesses: Premium subscription pricing and rigid API integration limits.

Security/Data: Standard cloud encryption; uses anonymized chat logs for public model improvement.

Vendor B: Aura Hospitality AI

Strengths: Enterprise-grade security, zero data-retention guarantees, excellent custom integration.

Weaknesses: Complex interface requiring significant staff training; slower customer support SLAs.

Security/Data: SOC2 compliant, isolated tenant architecture, GDPR ready.

Vendor C: OmniServe Enterprise

Strengths: Highly cost-effective subscription tier, broad feature set.

Weaknesses: Inconsistent vendor support response times, mixed user reviews on workflow reliability during peak transaction spikes.

Security/Data: Standard security standards; requires manual compliance review.

Vendor Scoring Matrix

Assign your criteria weights (1 = Low Priority, 5 = Critical Priority) and score each vendor from 1 to 10.
